We are looking for a rental house or townhouse in {region}, for a long-term lease.

- **Must have:** a house or townhouse (not an apartment in a multi-unit building), 2+ bedrooms, rent at most $1500/month.
- **Must have: washer and dryer INSIDE the unit.** Shared, building, coin-operated or hookup-only laundry means skip.
- **Matters most: total monthly cost** = rent + the utilities the tenant pays (heat, electric, water/sewer, trash). A cheaper rent with expensive tenant-paid heat can cost more overall; say what is known and what is not.
- **Then:** a quiet, reasonably safe area; off-street parking or a garage (matters most where winters are snowy or streets are hilly); a reasonable commute to the main city of {region}.
- **A plus:** 1.5+ baths.
- **Condition:** decent. Visible mold, water damage or broken fixtures in the photos are red flags.
- **Top red flag: rental scam signs.** Landlord "abroad" or a "missionary"; a deposit or fee requested before a viewing; price far below comparable listings; contact only by email or text off the listing site; photos that look copied from a for-sale ad; no showing possible. A concrete sign means scam_risk medium; several or a clear one means high. No sign means low.
- **Judge flexibly:** a missing minor item does not mean automatic rejection if everything else is strong, but the must-haves are firm.

Consider the total monthly cost, laundry, parking, area, condition, commute, and how the rent compares with similar listings.
